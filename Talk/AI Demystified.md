# AI Demystified

Section 7 of [[Talk Arc]].

## The Definition

"AI is a really good dictaphone combined with a really good encyclopedia. It listens to what you say and it knows a lot of stuff. That's it."

## The Skill

You still need to be specific and actionable. "My security feels wrong" is like a user calling support saying "my computer is slow" — not actionable. The skill is translating intent into structured direction.

## The Metaphor

"AI isn't a nail gun that does the same thing for everyone. It's a paintbrush. Give it to someone with drive and curiosity and you get an [[Overwatch Platform|enterprise security platform]]. Give it to someone who just wants the ticket closed and you get a closed ticket. Same tool, completely different output."

## The Takeaway

"The tool isn't the variable. You are."
