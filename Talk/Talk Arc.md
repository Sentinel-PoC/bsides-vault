# Talk Arc

9-section arc for "$200 and a Conversation" at BSides Fort Wayne, June 6, 2026.

## Flow

1. [[The Personal Story]] — "I felt gatekept."
2. [[What 200 Gets You]] — Show the [[Overwatch Platform]] live
3. [[The Live Demo]] — Build something with AI in front of the audience
4. [[The Cost Argument]] — $400-500k team vs $200/month
5. [[The Uncomfortable Truth]] — This cuts both ways (offensive use)
6. [[The Verification Problem]] — "Is this actually good?"
7. [[AI Demystified]] — "A really good dictaphone + encyclopedia"
8. [[Drive Is The Differentiator]] — AI promotes thinking, doesn't remove it
9. [[The Close]] — "The cost excuse is dead"

## Format

- 30 or 60 minute slot (expand [[The Live Demo]] and [[NIST 800-53 Compliance|NIST sections]] for 60)
- [[The Live Demo]] is the centerpiece — the workflow IS the message
- Pre-recorded TTS agent intros for each domain
- Questions throughout, not just at end
- Failure during demo is fine — catching it live proves the thesis

## Key Principle

"If it works perfectly, the audience thinks 'cool, AI is magic' and misses the point. If it breaks and I catch it live, that's the whole thesis — the human is the verification layer."
