# Q&A Themes

Things that don't go in the talk. Let the audience pull them out.

## Compliance Theater

Most compliance is paper that doesn't reflect reality. [[compliance-vault|Automated evidence collection]] removes the human with an incentive to fudge. See [[NIST 800-53 Compliance]].

## The Middle Layer Compression

AI compresses the organizational middle — Tier 3+ folks who translate management direction into implementation. They either move up into architecture/design or down into verification. The implementation layer is being automated. See [[Agent Teams]] for a live example.

## Resistance is Futile

Nobody resisted electricity, cars, washing machines, or computers successfully. Companies that refuse AI won't get outargued, they'll get outpriced. See [[The Cost Argument]].

## The Sandbox Path to Production

Start with AI in sandboxed dev environments. Over time, sandbox → dev → staging → production. Same pattern as ATMs, self-checkout, every automation adoption ever.

## The Steak Analogy

"This isn't filet mignon at filet prices. It's filet at New York strip prices." See [[The Cost Argument]].

## Experience Bias Cuts Both Ways

The senior engineer with 15 years of scar tissue might make better architectural decisions. Or they might be fighting the last war. Enterprise infrastructure is full of cargo cult decisions dressed up as experience. See [[The Verification Problem]].

## If My Work Isn't Good — That Proves the Point

"If someone pokes holes in my platform, that doesn't embarrass me. It proves my thesis in real time." See [[The Verification Problem]].
