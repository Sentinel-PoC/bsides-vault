# Drive Is The Differentiator

Section 8 of [[Talk Arc]].

## The Thesis

"AI doesn't remove thinking. It promotes our need to think, and at higher levels."

## The Scary Part

"If you don't want to do more, AI will just allow you to do less."

The friction of doing things manually used to force a minimum level of engagement. That friction was annoying but load-bearing. Now it's gone. Someone can produce output without understanding, close tickets without learning, pass audits without knowing what they're auditing.

## The Opportunity

Companies could pair junior people with [[Agent Teams|AI agents]] right now. Let them build, break things, let the agent catch what they miss. Better training than any $8,000 certification course. And you'd find out fast who has the drive to grow.

## The Line

"Most people will use AI to ignore harder."
